Notes and political data analysis

  • November 2026 · Working paper The mining agenda in the Chilean Congress: votes, parties, territory and changes in bill text, 2018–2026

    How much bills affecting mining change in Congress, what drives their pace and when deputies from mining regions break with their party. It covers the 2026 agenda and lithium policy, which was implemented without going through Congress.

  • November 2026 · Working paper Who leaves and what changes? Party exits on the Chilean right and voting in the Chamber of Deputies, 2014–2026

    Who left the UDI and RN to join the radical right. They already voted on the right flank of their party, especially on security and the economy, and their votes moved away from the party after they left, not before.

  • November 2026 · Working paper Beyond the rule: electoral and parliamentary fragmentation in Chile after the end of the binomial system, 2009–2026

    Why the Chilean Congress has twice as many effective parties today as in 2009. The vote dispersed, the new rule lets part of that dispersion through and more and more legislators switch party during their term.

  • November 2026 · Working paper Getting in and staying in: the double membership threshold of the political system reform and the 2028 party supply

    What the new membership requirements change for parties: an effective threshold 70% higher, four parties that do not currently reach eight regions and large parties that fall below the threshold in regions where they win much of their vote.

  • November 2026 · Working paper The end of entrenchment: term limits and municipal competition in Chile, 2012–2024

    What changed when Law No. 21,238 kept 165 mayors off the ballot in 2021 and 2024: more candidates where the mayor had been in office for years, and turnover that grows because seats open up, not because the outgoing camp is punished.

  • November 2026 · Working paper La Reina towards 2028: realignment, split-ticket voting and municipal scenarios

    What the 2016 to 2025 results say about La Reina's next municipal election: a mayoral vote that was not a coalition vote, neighbourhoods that resemble the adjacent municipalities and a council that depends on list coordination.
