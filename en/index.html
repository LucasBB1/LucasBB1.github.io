I am a political scientist, graduated with highest honours from Universidad Diego Portales, and I am currently studying for an MA in Political Science, with a concentration in Government and Public Policy, at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. I am interested in electoral behaviour, public opinion and the relationship between citizens and democratic institutions in Chile and Latin America, as well as in the design and evaluation of public policy.

My work combines quantitative analysis of political data with experience in government. I worked as an adviser in the Interministerial Coordination Division of the Ministry of the Interior, where I tracked more than 330 presidential commitments of the Ministries of Finance and of Culture, Arts and Heritage, together with the regional commitments and investment projects of the Maule and Ñuble regions, coordinating their territorial monitoring with regional authorities. Before that, I did my professional internship at the Ministry General Secretariat of the Presidency (SEGPRES).

Interests

Research agenda

My research studies the transformation of political representation in Chile, with a focus on electoral and party institutions, political competition and the behaviour of actors within representative institutions. I work with electoral and legislative data, quantitative designs and reproducible materials. The working papers published so far address that question at the municipal level, in the party system and in Congress. I also publish applied briefings on legislative and regulatory risk.
